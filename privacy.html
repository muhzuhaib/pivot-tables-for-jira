Privacy

Pivot Tables for Jira privacy policy

Your issue data stays inside your own Jira site. Nothing about it is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app reads

The app reads the Jira issues that match the query you type, using your own Jira permissions, through Atlassian's own APIs. It also reads your site's field list so the field picker can show your real fields. It is read-only: it never creates, changes or deletes anything in Jira.

Where that data goes

Nowhere. The issues are shown to you in your browser and are gone when you close the page. The app makes no connections to any server of ours, because there are none. There is no analytics, no tracking and no third party service of any kind in the app.

What is stored, and where

Saved pivot definitions (a name, your query, the field layout and the display choices) and your last used settings are stored in Atlassian's Forge storage for your site, inside Atlassian's cloud, under Atlassian's own terms. No issue data is stored there, only the definition of how to build the table. Deleting a saved pivot deletes it.

What we can see

Nothing about your issues. The only thing the Atlassian Marketplace reports to any app vendor is install counts and which versions of the app a site runs, and that is all we ever see.

Billing

Payment, invoicing and subscriptions are handled entirely by Atlassian. We never see your payment details and there is no card to enter anywhere in the app.
